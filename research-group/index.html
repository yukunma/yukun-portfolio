Yukun Ma Research Group

The Yukun Ma Research Group is founded and led by Principal Investigator Yukun Ma. We develop econometric methods that remain reliable in complex, high-dimensional, and weakly identified settings.

Research agenda

Our work is organized around three connected areas:

  • Robust inference: methods for clustered, multiway-clustered, and dyadic data.
  • Machine learning and high-dimensional econometrics: statistically valid estimation and inference with flexible nuisance models and many covariates.
  • Weak identification and overlap: reliable procedures for instrumental-variables and treatment-effect settings where standard approximations can fail.

The group combines econometric theory, computation, and applications. Current papers, publication links, and replication materials are collected on the Research page.
